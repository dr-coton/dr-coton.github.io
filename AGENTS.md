# Blog Writing

Before drafting, revising, or illustrating a blog article, read
`docs/writing-guide.md` and apply its editorial and animation guidance.
The user's current instructions take precedence. Treat the guide as a living
record of preferences, not a fixed article template.

Keep private project identities and data out of articles when anonymization is
requested. Separate verified implementation from illustrative examples and
unconfirmed author experience. Do not invent measurements or development history.

Drafts may be previewed locally when requested. Do not infer permission to publish
or push a new article from an earlier request to push unrelated changes.
